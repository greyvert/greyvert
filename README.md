### 🎨 About Me

I am a 3D Artist specializing in Hard Surface Modeling and Character Clothing / Outfits for games, cinematics, and digital art.

- 🔹 Specialization: Hard-surface props, military & sci-fi gear, detailed garments, high-to-low poly modeling workflow.
- 🔹 Key Focus: Clean sub-d topology, efficient UV unwrapping, detailed PBR texturing, and realistic cloth simulations.
- 🔹 Open to: GameDev projects, freelance, and studio opportunities.
